Who We Are

About Our Work

Through the work of Friends of CROSS, we raise funds to support Cancer Research and improve patient care across cancer care systems, including the Trinity St James Cancer Institute and St. James's Hospital.

Our fundraising activities support the education and training of high-quality medical students and allow for investments in state-of-the-art laboratory equipment to drive translational research projects aimed at the prevention, understanding, and treatment of various forms of cancer.

Friends of CROSS is a registered charity in Ireland — Charity Number 15364

Making a Difference

Our Impact

Through our fundraising activities, CROSS has raised funds to support key equipment in the Trinity College Dublin Dept. of Surgery at St. James's Hospital. This equipment is essential for the ongoing research of the department.

€1M+
Raised to purchase cutting-edge cancer research equipment

Equipment We've Funded

Equipment Photo

Flow Cytometer

Advanced cell analysis technology enabling researchers to identify and sort individual cells, critical for understanding cancer cell behaviour and developing targeted therapies.

Equipment Photo

Tissue Microarrayer

Precision instrument that allows researchers to analyse multiple tissue samples simultaneously, accelerating the pace of cancer research and biomarker discovery.

Equipment Photo

Cryostat

Essential laboratory equipment used to prepare thin tissue sections for microscopic examination, enabling detailed study of cancer tissue samples at extremely low temperatures.

Equipment Photo

PCR Thermal Cycler

Core molecular biology equipment used to amplify DNA segments, fundamental to genetic research into cancer mutations and the development of personalised medicine approaches.
